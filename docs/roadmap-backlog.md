## Practical Diagnostic — Conditional 1.3 Target

Start only after intended users demonstrate difficulty choosing a next action with the existing kit and guides. Test the advice manually first; a short decision guide may be sufficient.

- [ ] Confirm user need and test questions against real workflow examples.
- [ ] Ask only enough to suggest up to three actions linked to existing guidance; allow unknowns and a justified no-change recommendation.
- [ ] Keep answers local, make the interaction accessible, and support export without an account.
- [ ] Explain the source and limits of advice and its boundary with facilitated HELM Studio services.
- [ ] Test decision rules, including missing information and risky work; never infer employability or staffing cuts from answers.

**Publication gate:** Intended users can choose and explain a relevant next step; advice maps to canonical guidance, states uncertainty and passes worked examples; accessibility and data-handling checks pass. Implemented decision logic requires automated rule tests. A questionnaire or score is not itself evidence of useful advice.

## Release and Lifecycle Maintenance

Existing commands and procedures live in `deployment.md`, `updates.md` and `../CONTRIBUTING.md`. These recurring requirements are applied per release, not marked globally complete after one release:

- Freeze scope and explicitly move unfinished work; satisfy the selected milestone's gates with linked evidence.

## Explicitly Deferred

Retain these outside active delivery unless a documented need changes the decision:

- Additional foundational principles.
- A large static role-page collection without practical transition workflows.
- Accounts, cloud dashboards or a general-purpose SaaS platform.
- Certification programs or practitioner communities.
- Claims that HELM applies identically to every company function.
- A rewrite away from Astro.
- Complex personalization without a demonstrated diagnostic need.
- A universal runtime-agent platform or specialized staffing prescription for coding-agent adopters.
- Mandatory company-wide expansion.
- AGI arrival forecasts, universal future-job catalogs or automatic staffing recommendations from capability benchmarks.

Keep this backlog current when work is selected, completed or explicitly rejected. The historical snapshot preserves the earlier wording; this document owns the ongoing conditional work.

## Working with agents

Agents can change the amount and timing of work arriving for review. Check whether planning, review capacity and learning arrangements still fit the work.

Ask the people doing the work where it gets stuck. Look at quality, user outcomes and total effort alongside output volume before changing responsibilities or targets.

Keep time for coaching, independent practice and recovery skills. One person’s tool adoption does not determine the capability of the whole team.

## Experience to discuss

Discuss examples of planning, coaching and delivery responsibility. How did the person involve the team, handle a difficult trade-off and check the effect of a change? Use agent-assisted scenarios when they are relevant to the role.
